SINCE 2004, NEEDLESS HAS BEEN PUSHING THE BOUNDARIES OF EXTREME MUSIC, BLENDING INTRICATE COMPOSITIONS WITH RAW INTENSITY. WITH A SOUND THAT FUSES PROGRESSIVE, THRASH, AND DEATH METAL, THEY HAVE EMERGED AS ONE OF THE LEADING POWERHOUSES OF THE HUNGARIAN UNDERGROUND.

THE BAND'S SECOND ALBUM, THE COSMIC CAULDRON (2022), WAS A BREAKTHROUGH MOMENT, RELEASED UNDER UPRISING! RECORDS. THE RECORD GAINED INTERNATIONAL RECOGNITION, APPEARING IN MAJOR METAL WEBSHOPS, INCLUDING NUCLEAR BLAST AND NAPALM RECORDS, AND MAKING ITS WAY TO AUDIENCES FROM THE USA TO JAPAN. THE ENERGETIC LIVE PERFORMANCES HAVE ALSO SEEN THEM SHARE THE STAGE WITH RENOWNED ACTS SUCH AS UNLEASHED AND VEKTOR, WHILE ALSO TOURING ACROSS EUROPE.

NAVIGATING BETWEEN THEMES OF MYTHOLOGY, SCIENCE FICTION, AND DEEP PHILOSOPHICAL CONTEMPLATION, NEEDLESS IS NOW PRESENTING THE MOST AMBITIOUS RELEASE YET. THEIR THIRD ALBUM RELEASED ON MAY 30, 2025 VIA INERTIAL MUSIC (HOME OF WELL RENOWNED MUSICIANS LIKE NIKLAS SUNDIN), MARKS THE NEXT CHAPTER IN THEIR ARTISTIC EVOLUTION.
